Methodology

How We Rate Websites

Pythia™ analyzes 11 critical indices to produce a unified P-Score (0-100) and Pythia™ Rating (AAA-C). Think of it like a credit rating for websites.

The 11 Indices

1Speed - Page load performance, TTFB. Based on research showing 1% revenue loss per 100ms.
2Mobile - Mobile-first responsiveness, critical as mobile traffic dominates.
3SEO - Search optimization. Search traffic converts 2-6x more.
4Interactivity - User experience quality, JavaScript execution.
5Accessibility - Inclusive design, WCAG compliance, ADA requirements.
6Privacy - Security headers, HTTPS, GDPR compliance.
7CDN - Content delivery, caching strategy.
8Page Weight - File size optimization, mobile data costs.
9Modern Standards - WebP/AVIF, lazy loading, ES6.
10Code Quality - Maintainability, blocking resources.
11Sustainability - CO₂ per page view, green hosting.

Rating System

AAA (95-100) Outstanding
AA (90-94) Excellent
A (85-89) Very Good
BBB (80-84) Good
BB (75-79) Fair
B (70-74) Needs Improvement
CCC-C (0-69) Critical Issues

The Translation Problem

Bridging Technical Metrics and Executive Decision-Making

Pythia™ Research Team | December 2025

Abstract

Website performance impacts revenue (1% per 100ms), yet a translation problem exists: technical teams measure in milliseconds, executives need simple answers. This paper presents Pythia™, a standardized rating system (AAA-C) that unifies 11 performance indices, similar to how Moody's rates bonds.

Key Points

  • Problem: CIOs speak milliseconds, CMOs need "Is our site good enough?"
  • Current Solutions: PageSpeed/GTmetrix lack business context
  • Solution: Dual interface - Technical P-Score + Executive Rating
  • Validation: 15,000+ scans, high correlation with business metrics
  • Impact: Decision time reduced from weeks to days
