About Pythia

Pythia is The Digital Health Rating Agency — providing standardized, executive-friendly website health ratings.

The Problem

Executives can't assess digital health. There's no standardized rating system, technical metrics don't translate to business ROI, and digital health is invisible to the C-suite.

The Solution

Just like Moody's rates bonds, Pythia rates websites. We provide:

  • Dual-Persona Approach: Letter grades (AAA-C) for executives, P-Scores for developers
  • Competitive Intelligence: Instant competitive benchmarking
  • Standardized Ratings: Clear ROI signals for business decisions

Our Mission

To make digital health as understandable and actionable as financial credit ratings — empowering executives to make informed decisions about their digital investments.

Methodology

Physics-Aware Scoring

Pythia uses physics-aware scoring that separates:

  • Controllable factors: Rendering performance, optimization, code quality
  • Uncontrollable factors: Network latency (minimum 200ms due to physics)

We don't penalize websites for the speed of light. A 200ms baseline is applied before scoring begins.

Rating System

Pythia provides two rating formats:

Executive View - Credit Ratings:
  • 📊 AAA (95-100): Exceptional digital health
  • 📊 AA (90-94): Excellent performance
  • 📊 A (85-89): Strong performance
  • 📊 BBB (80-84): Good performance
  • 📊 BB (75-79): Fair performance
  • 📊 B (70-74): Below average
  • 📊 CCC (65-69): Poor performance
  • 📊 CC (60-64): Very poor
  • 📊 C (0-59): Critical issues
Developer View - P-Scores:

Numerical scores from 0-100 with detailed component breakdowns across 11 performance metrics.

11 Core Components

Our P-Score aggregates 11 weighted components:

  1. Karpov (25%): Load time, TTFB, resource count
  2. Tyche (20%): Script optimization
  3. Vortex (10%): Image optimization
  4. Nexus (8%): Mobile responsiveness
  5. Helix (7%): Privacy & security
  6. Pulse (6%): SEO foundations
  7. Nova (6%): CDN & caching
  8. Eden (6%): Page weight
  9. Aether (6%): Modern standards
  10. Quantum (5%): Meta-optimization
  11. Echo (1%): Console errors
